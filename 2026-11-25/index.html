Ten days after he announced he was done, the Wo Hop To figure withdrew his retirement in his own words — and by Tuesday his name was back where it has always sat: under Administration, on a Vice City roster.

The Commission’s View

A New City, the Same Record

The map changed this week. The record did not. Families who spent months saying goodbye to Los Santos are now posting from Vice City, and the first thing most of them posted was a roster, a flag, a photograph of the water. That is not nothing. A city is built out of exactly such small public acts, repeated until they harden into fact.

But the week’s real movement was quieter. A man took back his own retirement in his own words. Two families confirmed, in this paper’s own comments, a table that until then rested on one man’s announcement. A retirement date passed with no retirement. Claims made in the old city stand unanswered in the new one; changing the map does not settle them.

This paper takes no side in any of it. It will say only what it has always said: the story of this city is written by the people in it, in public, or it is written by whoever bothers to post. The Chronicle’s new bureau changes nothing about that. Viceport will be covered the way La Mesa was — from the record, and only from the record.

A Note on the Record

The Chronicle is written from the record families make themselves — their own posts, their own statements, their own words, given in their own places. This paper does not run from site to site piecing together what a family might be doing. If it is not on your record, it is not in this paper.

So the city writes its own story here. Post it, and it runs as given. Post nothing, and the story runs on what the other side put up.

Silence is not a correction.
