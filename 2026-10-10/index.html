Breaking developments in the Vencini case

Lead story

Vencini Case Widens on Two Fronts

A street conflict and a homicide defense are now moving forward in parallel.

Commission Desk • Filed Saturday afternoon

A revenge attempt aimed at a Vencini associate failed Friday, widening the dispute around a closely watched homicide case.

The strike was attributed to the person previously identified as the alleged victim. The Vencini side supplied the only account available by press time.

A defense attorney has taken the associate’s case, formally answering allegations that until now had played out largely through family statements and street action.

The feud remains unresolved outside the courtroom while the legal challenge begins inside it.

Underworld Report

New organizations and family movement

Andolini Family

Former Morello captain builds a new power center

The Andolini family has emerged under a founder described as a former Morello captain, extending an old connection into a separate organization.

The new family reports a roster of more than 50 members. That claimed strength gives the Andolini arrival unusual weight among the week’s new names and immediately places the organization among the larger groups operating in public.

Chicago Outfit

Another organization steps into public view

The Chicago Outfit has surfaced as a new presence, posting whack footage as its first visible signal of activity.

The organization has not yet published a full hierarchy or alliance structure. Its early material nevertheless marks a deliberate entrance into the city’s crime-family field.

Rozay Family

Council keeps the family banner visible

A Rozay council member returned the family name to public view while an allied organization warned that it would not avoid a challenge.

The paired messages amounted to a show of unity after a busy overnight run, keeping the Rozay family active in the wider commission conversation while senior leadership maintained a lower profile.
