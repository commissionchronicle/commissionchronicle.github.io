A Retirement — In His Own Words

The Castro Don Retires; the Chair Sits Empty

The Castro family’s own account announced on Thursday that its don is “retired for good in this community.” The successor he promised to name for the next city has not been named.

City Desk • Filed Tuesday night

Los Santos — The Castro don announced his retirement on Thursday, October 29, in a post on the Castro Crime Family’s own account, quoted as posted: “Would like it to be known that I am retired for good in this community and will be naming a new Don of The Castro Crime Family for gta 6, it was a pleasure meeting and experiencing this community with you all. Some bad, some good all in all it was fun, good luck everyone with gta 6 and hope to see the community turn around and thrive.” — signed by the Castro don.

This is a retirement on the family’s own word — confirmed as announced. What it is not, yet, is a succession: the new don he promised to name had not been named as of press close Tuesday night, November 3, and the family’s only post since was a captionless story on November 2.

The Castro chair stands empty in public.

No More Ceasefires

Muller Drops Its Ceasefires and Goes Back to War

In a post dated October 30, Muller announced it will sign no more ceasefires or alliances, will drop several it already holds, and returns to contracts and war. It named no partner and no target.

Los Santos — Muller announced the posture change on Friday, October 30, in a post quoted as posted: “Muller is taking no more ceasefires or alliances for the foreseeable future and will be dropping several ceasefires as we move towards gta6 muller will be going back into its old ways on gta6 where we will take on contracts and go to war with all families not with us nothing personal just business if u wish to solidify friendship and business with us onto gta6 reach out to us”.

The announcement is confirmed as made; its contents are a posture, not an act — no ceasefire partner named, no list published, no family named as a target. The same day, October 30, Muller posted of leaving this city for a new path in Vice City. Until a named party speaks, the row stands in Watching, not At War.

An Alliance — One Voice So Far

O’Rilley Names Itself to the Triad Alliance; the Triad Side Is Silent

The alliance of the Triads and the Irish Mob was first announced by the Wo Hop To side on October 10. What is new is O’Rilley’s October 31 post naming itself to it and claiming it is now solidified on PlayStation — that step stands on O’Rilley’s word only.

Los Santos — The O’Rilley head published on Saturday, October 31: “The O’Rilley Irish Mob × Wo Hop To Triads — An alliance established on Xbox, now solidified on PlayStation. Two organisations, bound by mutual respect and a shared understanding. A man’s word carries weight, and true alliances are built on honour, not promises alone. Respect earned. Word honoured.”

The alliance itself is not new: announced by the Wo Hop To side October 10 and restated in this paper’s October 21 Sit-Down. The Triad side’s posts of October 31 and November 1 are roster listings that do not mention O’Rilley; Wo Hop To marked its 17th anniversary on November 2 with no acknowledgment of the alliance; Triad stories of November 3 named no partner.

O’Rilley also announced October 31 that two more brothers had taken their place within the family, names not given. If the Triad side confirms the PlayStation step, the paper will print it; until then it runs as claimed.

The Sit-Down

A weekly conversation with the figures running Los Santos

De Luca

Godfather of the De Luca Crime Family

Answered via DM, October 28, 2026.

In character, for the record — introduce yourself: your name in the life, and your position in the family.

My Name is Rocco De Luca but I’m better known as Doom. I’m the Godfather of my family.

Every family has an origin story. What’s yours — how did this family come up in Los Santos?

I came into this life in early 2023 as a low-level associate of the Spietato Sovrano Crime Family. I soaked up all the knowledge I could from my Don and worked my way up the ranks until the day came when I was honored to take up the reigns of Don of the family. After retiring my seat at the Don’s table I decided to start my own legacy and I started the De Luca Crime family and later the Black Haven Mob. Both of these families are built upon the foundation that I learned in Sovrano. Loyalty, Integrity, and Intelligence.

What’s the biggest misconception people have about your family?

I would say lately that the biggest misconception is that we don’t get active in the streets. Anyone who knows the history of any family I’ve been connected to would know that we move strategically. I’ve never believed in senseless fighting but we’ve absolutely been in more than a couple fights we just believe there’s more to this life than that.

The city’s been loud lately — alliances, hits, fallouts. Where does your family stand in all of it?

My family has a long standing of mostly being independent. I will say lately tho that my family stands strong in our conviction of no tolerance towards Racism. If you understand that we are cool, if you don’t then you know where you stand with us.

Walk me through a night that defines what your family is about.

There is no one single night that defines what my family is about. We pride ourselves on great content and we try to tell our stories through the content we post. Check out our Instagram page and you will see our story displayed better than I could ever describe it.

Loyalty gets talked about a lot in this life. What does it actually look like day to day?

Loyalty is a dime a dozen in this life. It’s talked about but not many truly exhibit real loyalty. Loyalty is even though we don’t agree I still respect you. Loyalty is even if I’m not apart of your family I still show love and give you the same respect that I would if I was apart of your family.

What’s the one rule in your family that nobody breaks — and what happens if they do?

Don’t go against the family. If you do this you will be met with nothing but hostility.

Last one — where is this family a year from now?

This family will be a consistent staple in the mafia community. One of respect and honor.

In Conclusion

“Brute force is just a confession of poor planning. Anyone can kick down a door; a true Strategist owns the building before the foundation is even laid.”

The Commission’s View

The Doors of the City

This was a week of doors. A don in Los Santos announced, in his own words, that he is retired for good, and left his family’s chair empty for a successor he has not yet named. Another house announced it will sign no more ceasefires and will let several existing ones lapse, returning to contracts and war as the city counts down to a new one. An alliance was claimed solidified in a single voice, while the other side of it marked an anniversary and said nothing about it at all.

The Chronicle takes no side in any of it. But it notices the pattern. As the migration everyone talks about draws closer, the city’s powers are deciding what they intend to carry with them — and what, and whom, they intend to leave behind. A retirement, a dropped ceasefire, an unanswered claim: each is a small announcement about what the next city will be allowed to remember of this one.

A city is measured by what its people do on their way out the door. The Chronicle will be watching who walks through, and who is left standing in the room.
