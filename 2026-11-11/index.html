The Departure — In Its Own Words

Marzoni Has Left Los Santos

The family page announced Tuesday that the Marzoni Famiglia has left Los Santos behind for Vice City — after a week of claims filed against it and answers filed back on film, the departure itself was the answer.

City Desk • Filed Tuesday night

Los Santos — The Marzoni family page announced on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, in the past tense: “The Marzoni Famiglia has left Los Santos behind. Vice City, the family is coming.” The family is gone from this city on its own word — announced, and printed as announced.

The week that preceded it was loud. In a reel dated Thursday, November 5, 2026, the Muller family claimed the assassination of a Marzoni figure — claimed, Muller side only. The account carrying the target’s name posted on November 7 announcing a return from a leave of absence — noted here as activity, not a denial.

In a story dated Saturday, November 8, 2026 (12:06 AM PT), the Capone godfather’s account said the Capone family officially calls out Marzoni to a CB or PFMW at a time and date of Capone’s choosing; no date was set. Hours later, in a story dated Saturday, November 8, 2026 (8:17 AM PT), a Neopolo figure posted “Marzoni down” — no figure, killer, cause or circumstances named.

Marzoni’s answers came on film. In a reel dated Friday, November 7, 2026, a Marzoni figure claimed the whack of a figure linked on this paper’s record to Lucianno and Vinci — and that figure was publicly active on November 9 without addressing the claim. A second Marzoni-authored film dated November 7, 2026 reported the Vinci godfather found dead: the film reports the death and does not claim Marzoni carried it out, and no Vinci-side answer is on file. A film hand-delivered to this paper on November 8, 2026 claimed the assassination of a Muller figure — claimed, Marzoni side only, no Muller answer on file.

On Monday, November 9, 2026, the Marzoni godfather said in a story the Famiglia is focusing on itself “without any problems from LCN” and invited independent families who want to work with Marzoni in Vice City to reach out; the family page the same day posted a member handling family business “out east.” In a post dated Tuesday, November 10, 2026, the Marzoni godfather wrote of a future where the family “thrives and prospers without any oversight from other people and or crews.”

No formal Marzoni answer to the Capone callout or the Neopolo claim appeared before press. The family’s answer, in the end, was the departure itself.

The Commission’s View

Silence Is Not an Answer

This week Los Santos spoke almost entirely in claims. A godfather, an underboss, a family’s soldier, three men in a single reel — each death announced by the side claiming it, and answered, in every case, by silence. The targets posted nothing. Their families posted nothing. The city was left holding a pile of announcements and no replies.

This paper prints claims as claims, and it will keep doing so. But a claim is the start of a record, not the record itself. Silence is not confirmation, and it is not denial either — it is a family choosing, for its own reasons, not to spend its answer in public. Some of those reasons are strategy. Some are contempt. From the outside, they look exactly alike.

Meanwhile, the one family that did answer did so without answering at all: it packed, declared its business with the old city finished, and left for Leonida. In a week of announcements, the loudest statement was a departure.

The Chronicle will be watching who follows — and who, eventually, bothers to reply.
