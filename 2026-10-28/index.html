The Sit-Down

A weekly conversation with the figures running Los Santos

Vercetti

Godfather of the Vercetti Crime Family

Answered via DM, October 21, 2026.

In character, for the record — introduce yourself: your name in the life, and your position in the family.

My name is Walker Vercetti, Godfather of the Vercetti Crime Family.

Every family has an origin story. What’s yours — how did this family come up in Los Santos?

My good friend Swiss and I joined the mafia community in 2023. Our first home was the Bellic Crime Family, where we worked our way up through the ranks. I eventually reached the position of Capo. When the Don of Bellic left the family, I was voted in by my peers to succeed him. I stepped into that role and took on the responsibility of leading the family forward. That was the turning point. We rebranded as the Vercetti Crime Family, and that’s where my legacy began.

What’s the biggest misconception people have about your family?

I’m not sure there’s a particular misconception about our family. We’ve always been transparent in our dealings, shown respect where it was given, and made an effort to resolve issues through dialogue before resorting to violence. We’ve always strived to conduct ourselves honorably in the eyes of our peers within the mafia community. For a long time, the Vercetti Crime Family stood on its own. We never felt the need to attach ourselves to a faction to establish our identity or prove our worth. More recently, we’ve chosen to call LCN home. We see it as an opportunity to contribute to something bigger than ourselves and play a part in its continued success.

The city’s been loud lately — alliances, hits, fallouts. Where does your family stand in all of it?

I’ve always believed that taking a position on matters that don’t affect me, my family, or our reputation can be foolish. Sometimes, it’s better to stay out of the noise and focus on yourself and the people who depend on you. Not every conflict requires our involvement, and not every situation calls for us to choose a side. My position is clear: I stand with the Vercetti Family and the families of LCN. Nothing more.

Walk me through a night that defines what your family is about.

A night with the Vercetti Family is about laughter, bonding, great roleplay, and exceptional content. At the end of the day, this is what we’ve always been about. It’s about the experiences we create together, the relationships we build, and the memories we leave behind in the community. That’s what the Vercetti Family has always done, and it’s what we’ll continue to do.

Loyalty gets talked about a lot in this life. What does it actually look like day to day?

Loyalty shows itself when you stick around through the difficult times, not just when things are easy. Anyone can stand beside you when everything is going well. What matters is who’s still there when things get tough. That’s when you find out where someone’s loyalty truly lies.

What’s the one rule in your family that nobody breaks — and what happens if they do?

Deceit and treachery are the cardinal sins in the Vercetti Family. We treat every member with respect, provide opportunities for growth, and make sure everyone has a voice. I believe in giving people the chance to grow and proving that leadership doesn’t have to come at the expense of respect. But don’t mistake kindness for weakness. If someone takes advantage of the trust and respect I give them, I’ll handle the situation accordingly. Deceit and betrayal have consequences, and neither is something I take lightly.

Last one — where is this family a year from now?

A year from now, I want the Vercetti name to represent hope and to be known for the positive impact we’ve had on the community. I want people to hear our name and have good things to say about how we’ve conducted ourselves. I want the family to be stronger, continuing to build on what we’ve established, and for our name and values to outlive my time in the community. Vercetti Arms will remain a core part of what we do. Excellence Without Compromise has been our motto for a long time, and we know what we’re good at. We’re also helping lay the foundation for LCN’s future and looking ahead to what GTA 6 will bring. We’re excited for the new chapter beginning next month. Ultimately, I want to see our members grow into leaders and establish families of their own, carrying the Vercetti way forward. If a year from now we’re still building, contributing, and helping the community grow, then we’re moving in the right direction.

In conclusion — “A man’s time in this life is limited. His name, his principles, and the legacy he leaves behind should outlive him.”

The Commission’s View

The City Underneath

This city has built itself a second city, and it fits in a phone. In that one, everything is a performance — every night staged, every slight broadcast, every opinion posted before it has been thought. It runs loud, around the clock, and it never sleeps, because it is terrified of being forgotten.

Underneath it, the first city is still there. It opens its hoods in parking lots at midnight. It fills a lobby on a Tuesday because somebody cared enough to organize it. It remembers who showed up, who helped push a stranger’s car, who stayed after the meet ended. None of that trends. All of it lasts.

A place is not what it posts about itself. It is what its people do when there is nothing to gain from being seen doing it. The noise will keep coming; it always does. But when this city’s story gets told years from now, it will not be the loudest version that survives. It will be the one that was actually lived.
